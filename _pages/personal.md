## Cooking & Baking

Cooking and baking are my creative outlets, allowing me to experiment and create dishes that bring joy to those around me. There’s something incredibly fulfilling about crafting a meal from scratch and sharing it with friends and family. My goal is to perfect my skills and master both the art and science of the kitchen, one recipe at a time.
