Faisal Mahmud, **Bo Shao**, Jonatan Aquino Rivas & Leila Hajibabai. Presented by Faisal Mahmud on May 17, 2026 in session LSC-22: Humanitarian Supply Chains 4 at the IISE Annual Conference & Expo 2026, Arlington, TX.

[[Session page]](https://iise.secure-platform.com/annual2026/solicitations/102014/sessiongallery/schedule/items/95324/application/17901) [[Full paper]](https://www.proquest.com/docview/3374569872)

This work develops an optimization framework for distributing multi-category food from NC State's Feed the Pack pantry directly to residence halls, subject to food inventory, category-specific demand, and volunteer vehicle capacity. The model is solved with Dantzig–Wolfe decomposition combined with capacity-constrained Clarke–Wright route generation.
